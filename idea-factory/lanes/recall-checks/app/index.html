Every number here counts NHTSA safety campaigns that apply to the year, make and model. A previous owner may already have had the work done, and some campaigns cover only a build-date window, so the true count for a given car is usually lower.

The free VIN-level answer is NHTSA's own lookup at nhtsa.gov/recalls (up to 100 VINs at a time) or the manufacturer's dealer portal. This page tells you which cars are worth that minute.

Severity is our reading of NHTSA's component and consequence text: critical is a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory, or brakes, steering, air bags, fuel or fire; serious is seat belts, wheels, suspension, hood or latch, stalling or rollaway; moderate is everything else.
