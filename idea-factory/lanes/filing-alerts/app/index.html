What this is, and what it is not

An 8-K is the notice a public company must file with the SEC within four business days of a major event. This page lists the ones filed by companies with a Michigan business address, and turns the SEC's item codes into plain words.

The plain-words line is a translation of the item codes, not a summary of the document. Open the filing for the details. The SEC already offers free per-company alerts; this adds a Michigan-wide view and a watchlist.
